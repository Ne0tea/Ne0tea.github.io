## Abstract

We assembled 67 rice genomes with >800 fully resolved centromeres, quantified centromeric local homogenization and multi-layer nested architecture, characterized structural variation and transposon-driven innovation, and proposed a new evolutionary model — **RICE** — for rice centromeres.

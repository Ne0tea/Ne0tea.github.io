## Abstract

Centromeres are pivotal chromosomal regions whose repetitive nature has long hindered systematic dissection. We assembled 67 rice genomes with more than 800 fully resolved centromeres, quantified the local homogenization and multi-layer nested architecture, characterized centromeric structural variation and transposon-driven innovation, and proposed a new evolutionary model — **RICE** — for rice centromeres.

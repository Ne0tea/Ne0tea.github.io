## Abstract

*Digitaria sanguinalis* is a globally noxious weed with widespread herbicide resistance, yet the genomic basis of its adaptability remains unclear. We assembled high-quality genomes of *D. sanguinalis* and its relatives and analysed 579 accessions sampled across China. Combined with herbicide dose–response assays, our results show that introgression contributes to local adaptation and underpins herbicide resistance in this weed.
